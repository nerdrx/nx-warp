Generated by `rc/sim/nxvc-rcsim`.  Do not edit by hand.

Grid: 34 x 34 tiles per eye, 2 eyes, 2312 tiles per stereo frame, 72 Hz.
Scene: sky (flat), walls and ground (band-limited texture), geometry
outlines (edges), a foliage band (high-activity texture, moving) and a
6x4-tile UI panel with the stencil bit set.  Two scene cuts, at frames
120 and 260.  Fixed foveation, no eye tracking.

Coded QP alone is misleading: a texture tile at res 1/4 with the
low-pass matrix has a *lower* coded QP than a text tile at full
resolution, because the ladder took its detail away instead of its
precision.  The comparable number is the effective QP, the coded QP
minus everything the ladder and the foveation resolution bought back
(RATECONTROL.md 4.6).  Lower is better; text must stay lowest.

## 7. Reading the numbers

See docs/RATECONTROL.md for the algorithms and the appendix for the
decisions these numbers were produced under.  Everything here is a
model: the per-tile cost function is the same `a * s^2 * gain *
2^(-QP/6)` the controller assumes, with a per-tile ground truth the
controller does not know and +/-8% noise.  It exercises the control
loop, not the codec; the gain constants in RateConfig are the numbers
a real encoder run has to replace.
