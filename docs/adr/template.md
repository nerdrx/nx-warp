## Context

What forces are in play. What is being traded against what. Numbers, with their source, and a label
saying whether they are measured or a design estimate. If this decision reconciles two sections of
the paper that disagreed, say what each said.

## Decision

One paragraph, in the present tense, stating exactly what the project does. Then the specifics:
constants, field widths, thresholds. This section should be quotable in the normative documents.

## Consequences

What becomes true, good and bad. What other decisions this forces. What it costs in bits, in
milliseconds, or in code that must now exist. What must be measured to find out whether the decision
was right, and in which phase.

## Alternatives considered

Each rejected option with the reason it lost. An alternative with no stated reason is not a rejected
alternative, it is an unexamined one.

## References

Paper sections, prior art, related ADRs.
