Method: host preparation validates the ASTC magic, block dimensions, width, height, depth, and exact raw length, strips only the 16-byte file header, then generates v2 full-block and v4 14-byte/block Zstd level-3 payloads. Identical payload files were pushed to Pico and SHA256-checked there. A scratch copy of `common/nxastc_packet_decode.h` adds an optional `ZSTD_DCtx*` (default null); current production packet parsing, frame strictness, declared content-size/exact-length checks, and v4 in-place expansion remain used. Each case has 20 warmups + 200 measured decodes per path, alternating path order. Timed region is `decode_payload`; packet parsing and byte-exact output comparison are outside timing.
