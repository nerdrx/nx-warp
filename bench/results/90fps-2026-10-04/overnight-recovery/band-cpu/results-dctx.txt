Matched reused-ZSTD_DCtx follow-up (original one-shot-decompress results remain in results.txt)
Date: 2026-10-04
Each whole decoder and each independent-region decoder reuses a dedicated ZSTD_DCtx allocated before the timed loop; partial and comparator paths likewise have separate reused contexts. Each context receives 20 warmups and 200 measured calls. Output and byte-exact validation remain after each timed interval. Square region benchmark includes region decompression plus scatter merge; horizontal band benchmark decompresses directly into contiguous final ASTC output rows (no merge copy). PC/Pico timings use the same harness shape; no GPU/network memory timing.

Takeaway: matched contexts substantially reduce the earlier square-region penalty, but 256px square decode+scatter remains materially slower on Pico; 128px remains worse. Full-width bands are near whole-frame decode cost at all tested sizes, with no scatter copy. Holding the final edge band (5.88% of the image) removes about 5.7-6.2% estimated wire bytes in this fixture pair and measures 20-61us faster than full forest decode on Pico, but retains previous-frame pixels in that bottom strip. Packet framing estimate is 8B per band plus standard 24B whole header; actual transport overhead/loss behavior is not implemented here. These timings do not prove live-network/render net benefit.
