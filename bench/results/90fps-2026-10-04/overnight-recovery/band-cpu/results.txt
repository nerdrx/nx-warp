Method: Zstd3 whole ASTC frame vs independently Zstd3-compressed regions gathered as contiguous ASTC blocks. Exact region decodes are merged into the proper 16B/block texture layout and byte-compared to input. Pixel edge regions are cropped at image bounds (2176/256 = 8 full + 128px edge; 2176/128 = 17 exact regions). Partial path retains dark-q6 as old buffer and decodes selected forest-q6 regions into it, skipping each 10th region; expected mixed buffer is byte-compared. Max tile scratch is allocated once outside timings. Decompress+merge timed; whole decode timed as comparator. Header sizes are estimates only and excluded from timed decode: 24B whole packet vs 8B/region. Each benchmark has 20 warmups + 200 measured iterations; whole and region/full order alternates each iteration, and full vs partial order alternates. Host and Pico perform compression/fixture setup before timing; this measures CPU vector memory, not mapped GPU memory, network or transport scheduling.
